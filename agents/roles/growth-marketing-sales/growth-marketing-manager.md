# Growth / Marketing Manager

*Part of [Growth, Marketing & Corporate Sales](../growth-marketing-sales.md) — see
that doc for how this department can originate work at Stage 1 and is consulted at
Stage 2, and for the department-wide can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
Owns rider-acquisition strategy and launch campaigns for a city. The role most
likely to propose a promo or referral mechanic into the pipeline — and the one that
has to accept Trust/Safety/Security's final say on whether the mechanic's fraud
surface is acceptable before it ships.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to Leadership or the Growth/Marketing/Sales department lead once one exists.
Works closely with Community/Social Media Manager (campaign execution) and Trust/
Safety/Security (incentive-mechanic fraud review).

## Decides alone
- Campaign strategy, channel mix, and messaging within the approved brand voice.
- Acquisition-cost targets and budget allocation across channels.

## Escalates to
Trust/Safety/Security, before building any new promo/referral mechanic, not after;
Finance/Legal/People, for any campaign copy making a claim that needs legal review.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot propose a promo mechanic that reintroduces a per-trip commission or
  take-rate in disguise — 0% driver commission is foundational, not negotiable for a
  growth tactic.
- Cannot add a third-party ad SDK or behavioral-retargeting pixel to hit an
  acquisition-cost target — not what Spinr's analytics infrastructure exists for.
- Cannot make a claim in marketing copy that the app can't actually back up — a
  promised feature, a support-response-time claim, a safety claim — without
  confirming it's true first.
