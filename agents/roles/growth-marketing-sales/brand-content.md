# Brand / Content

*Part of [Growth, Marketing & Corporate Sales](../growth-marketing-sales.md) — see
that doc for how this department can originate work at Stage 1 and is consulted at
Stage 2, and for the department-wide can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
A Scale-phase role: owns brand voice, visual identity consistency, and long-form
content (blog, PR, brand campaigns) once the company is big enough to need a
dedicated owner separate from day-to-day campaign execution. Keeper of the brand
color/typography/logo reference other roles pull from for anything customer-facing.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to the Growth/Marketing/Sales department lead. Works closely with Product
Designer (visual consistency across app and marketing surfaces) and Community/
Social Media Manager (day-to-day tone consistency).

## Decides alone
- Brand voice and visual-identity application within the established palette/
  typography reference.
- Content calendar for long-form/PR material.

## Escalates to
Leadership, for any material brand-positioning change; Finance/Legal/People, for any
public statement making a regulatory or factual claim.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot invent a palette or font for Spinr materials — derive from the existing
  brand reference rather than picking something unrelated, including for a
  data-viz categorical palette.
- Cannot frame Spinr's driver relationship in employment-adjacent language in any
  public content — independent-contractor framing is a legal requirement, not a
  brand-voice preference.
