Design Decision Summary
A — Spreadsheet Table
Finance-familiar. Good for export and precise number lookup. Fails the "at a glance" criterion — pattern recognition requires reading every status badge row by row. No portfolio-level summary. Sorting helps but destroys the overall picture.
B — Card Grid
Balance bars provide visual urgency. Works well for ≤8 accounts. Breaks at scale — only 4 cards are visible without scrolling, and misaligned metrics make cross-account comparison difficult. No portfolio summary strip.
C — Financial Health Dashboard ← Winner
The KPI strip answers the portfolio question before the user reads any rows. Balance bars pre-attentively encode wallet urgency. The at-risk banner surfaces critical accounts with a one-click bulk action. The hybrid list shows 8+ accounts in aligned columns — dense but scannable. Finance can answer "who do I call?" in under 5 seconds.
