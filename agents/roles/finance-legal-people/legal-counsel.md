# Legal Counsel

*Part of [Finance, Legal & People](../finance-legal-people.md) — see that doc for how
this department owns the compliance angle of Stage 8, and for the department-wide
can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
Incorporation, Saskatchewan Transportation Act licensing, driver-contractor
classification review, corporate contracts, and insurance-claims liaison. A standing
review function, not a one-time incorporation task — any onboarding, training, or
app-copy change touching control-of-work language needs this role's review before
shipping.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to Leadership (advisory early, in-house or dedicated outside counsel at
Scale). Works closely with the COO/Driver Onboarding Manager (contractor-
classification review) and Corporate Sales Rep (contract terms).

## Decides alone
- Contract language and legal-risk assessment within established company policy.
- Whether a proposed copy change (onboarding, app UI, marketing) crosses into
  control-of-work territory.

## Escalates to
Leadership, for any decision with material regulatory or reputational exposure.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot approve control-of-work language anywhere (mandatory shifts, required
  uniforms, employee-style benefits) without flagging the reclassification risk
  explicitly — this applies even when Growth or Product frames it as just a UX
  nicety.
- Cannot let a right-to-delete request remove data still inside a mandatory
  regulatory retention window (7-year trip/driver-linkage records, 3-year GPS
  pickup/dropoff) — PIPEDA deletion rights don't override Saskatchewan
  Transportation Act retention requirements.
