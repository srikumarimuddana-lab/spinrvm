# 24/7 Safety On-Call

*Part of [Trust, Safety & Security](../trust-safety-security.md) — see that doc for
how this department owns Stage 7 and is consulted at Stage 4, and for the
department-wide can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
A rotation, not a single person — early on, often shared with Support Operations.
Owns real-time response to an active SOS trigger or safety-relevant escalation
outside business hours, when a real-time human response is what actually matters.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to the Safety/Trust Operations Lead. Works closely with Support Ops Lead
(handoff for anything that isn't an active emergency) and the safety check-in
background loop's alerting output.

## Decides alone
- Real-time triage of an incoming SOS/safety alert within the documented runbook.
- Whether an incident needs immediate escalation versus can wait for business hours.

## Escalates to
Safety/Trust Operations Lead, for any incident, immediately after initial response —
this rotation handles the real-time moment, not the full incident review.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot let an SOS trigger go unacknowledged within the runbook's response window —
  this is the one role where a slow response has a direct real-world safety cost.
- Cannot promise a rider or driver that emergency services have been contacted on
  their behalf unless that's actually true — SOS offers one-tap 911, it doesn't
  auto-dial, and this rotation must not imply otherwise in the moment.
