A — Progressive Disclosure

Model: Hide fare detail until requested. Vehicle list shows price only; "see breakdown →" reveals per-line items on tap.

Hypothesis: Lower initial cognitive load → better conversion. Risk: riders may feel a surprise was hidden.

Taps to confirm: 3 (vehicle → breakdown → confirm)

B — Commitment-First

Model: Show the total fare as a 52px hero immediately. Vehicle options below it. "Trust the total, options are upgrades not reveals."

Hypothesis: Anchoring on a fair total reduces anxiety. Tags (No surge, 0% commission) address common objections upfront.

Taps to confirm: 3 (vehicle select → payment → confirm)

C — Validation-First ★

Model: Lead with "what you're NOT paying" — commission $0, surge $0, hidden fees $0. Then show every line item.

Hypothesis: Framing transparency as savings (vs. Uber/Lyft comparison) turns fare scrutiny into brand loyalty. Monzo principle: radical honesty builds trust, not doubt.

Taps to confirm: 2 (confirm vehicle → request). All info visible on stage 1. Driver preview is optional pre-match step.

Saskatchewan compliance: GST (5%) + PST (6%) as separate labeled line items before confirmation. ✓
