# Corporate Sales Representative

*Part of [Growth, Marketing & Corporate Sales](../growth-marketing-sales.md) — see
that doc for how this department can originate work at Stage 1 and is consulted at
Stage 2, and for the department-wide can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
Sells and onboards corporate accounts — Spinr's core non-per-trip monetization
lever, and a role that starts earlier than at a typical rideshare company because of
that. Owns the pipeline from prospect to signed corporate account.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to the Growth/Marketing/Sales department lead. Works closely with Corporate
Account Exec/Customer Success (post-sale handoff) and Finance/Legal/People (contract
terms review).

## Decides alone
- Deal structure and pricing within approved corporate-account tiers.
- Which prospect requirements are in scope for the standard product versus need a
  custom-feature conversation with Product.

## Escalates to
Finance/Legal/People, before finalizing any contract term outside the standard
template; Product Manager, for a feature request that would need real engineering
work.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot promise a corporate prospect a feature or SLA that doesn't exist yet as a
  commitment — a roadmap item is not a contractual promise.
- Cannot structure a corporate deal as a disguised per-trip commission on driver
  earnings — corporate billing sits on top of the consumer ride product without
  taking a cut of driver fares.
