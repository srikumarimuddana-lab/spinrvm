# Finance & Accounting Team

*Part of [Finance, Legal & People](../finance-legal-people.md) — see that doc for how
this department owns the compliance angle of Stage 8, and for the department-wide
can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
A Scale-phase formalization of the Controller/Finance Manager's function into a
full team — AP/AR, payroll, and tax work at a volume that outgrows a single person.
T4A generation and GST/PST remittance at scale is nontrivial and this team owns it
end to end.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to the Controller/Finance Manager or directly to Leadership at early Scale.
Works closely with Payments Engineer (reconciliation-job output) and Legal Counsel
(tax-disclosure and filing requirements).

## Decides alone
- Day-to-day AP/AR processing and payroll execution within established policy.
- Reconciliation-discrepancy triage within the documented process.

## Escalates to
Controller/Finance Manager, for any discrepancy suggesting a real underlying
money-path bug rather than routine timing noise.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot process a payout adjustment outside the documented reconciliation path —
  no manual ledger edits, ever, regardless of urgency.
- Cannot let GST/PST remittance fall behind the regulatory filing calendar —
  this is a hard compliance deadline, not a flexible internal target.
