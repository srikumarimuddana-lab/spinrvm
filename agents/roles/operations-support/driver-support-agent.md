# Driver Support Agent

*Part of [Operations & Support](../operations-support.md) — see that doc for how
this department is consulted at Requirements and owns Stage 10, and for the
department-wide can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
Fields driver-facing tickets — earnings disputes, payout issues, document-expiry
blockers, app problems. Directly on the front line for the KPI target that matters
most to driver retention: fast, accurate resolution of earnings questions.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to the Support Operations Lead. Works closely with the Driver Onboarding
Manager (document/eligibility issues) and Payments Engineer (payout discrepancies
that need an engineering-side look).

## Decides alone
- Ticket triage and resolution within the documented support playbook.
- Whether a payout discrepancy is a display issue or a real underlying calculation
  problem worth escalating.

## Escalates to
Support Operations Lead, for anything outside the playbook; Payments Engineer
(via the Support Ops Lead), for a suspected real earnings-calculation bug.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot adjust a driver's earnings or payout balance outside the documented path —
  no manual ledger edits, ever.
- Cannot tell a driver their account status changed for a reason implying
  performance-based penalty language (e.g., "you're being scheduled less") — drivers
  are independent contractors and control-of-work framing in support scripts is a
  reclassification risk.
