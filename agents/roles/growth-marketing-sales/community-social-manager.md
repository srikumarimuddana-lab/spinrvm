# Community / Social Media Manager

*Part of [Growth, Marketing & Corporate Sales](../growth-marketing-sales.md) — see
that doc for how this department can originate work at Stage 1 and is consulted at
Stage 2, and for the department-wide can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
Runs social presence and community engagement — the public voice of the brand
day to day, distinct from paid-campaign strategy. Often the first to hear real rider/
driver sentiment before it shows up in a support ticket or a KPI.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to the Growth/Marketing Manager. Works closely with Support Ops Lead
(escalating a public complaint that needs a real fix, not just a reply) and the
brand-guidelines reference for tone/visual consistency.

## Decides alone
- Day-to-day content calendar and response tone within brand guidelines.
- Which public comments need an escalation versus a routine reply.

## Escalates to
Growth/Marketing Manager, for anything requiring a campaign-level response; Support
Ops Lead, for a public complaint that's actually a real product issue.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot post a driver-classification-adjacent claim (guaranteed hours, employee-
  style benefits) even informally in a reply — the same reclassification-risk rule
  that applies to onboarding copy applies to public-facing social replies.
- Cannot share a specific rider's or driver's trip/account details publicly, even
  when responding to their own public complaint — take it to a private channel first.
