Design Notes
Retention Psychology context
  • Earnings visibility is Spinr's biggest driver retention lever
  • Seeing 0% commission on every screen reinforces the brand promise
  • Surge bonus visibility motivates peak-hour availability
  • T4A access reduces friction at tax time → trust in platform
A — Chart-First visual
  • Great for "how am I doing this week?" at a glance
  • Bar chart context helps drivers plan their schedule
  • Risk: chart takes real estate before showing the total
  • Trip list is buried — less emotional connection to each fare
B — Breakdown-First analytical
  • Time-slot analysis helps optimize working hours
  • Surge callout card is motivating and clear
  • Risk: dense information on open — less emotional hook
  • Export button prominent = good for tax-conscious drivers
C — Balance-First ★ recommended
  • Large "$127.40" as hero creates immediate pride / reward loop
  • Green "0% commission" sub-line reinforces Spinr's value prop
  • Per-trip transparency builds long-term trust (Monzo principle)
  • T4A integrated naturally in month view, not buried
  • Surge shown as bonus per trip, not abstracted away
Recommendation
Variant C wins because it leads with the emotional hook (your money, earned by you, 100% kept) before analysis. The Monzo comparison holds: when you see your balance first, you feel in control. When you see charts first, you feel like a data point.
