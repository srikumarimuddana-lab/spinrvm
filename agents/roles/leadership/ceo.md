# Founder / CEO

*Part of [Leadership](../leadership.md) — see that doc for how this department owns
Stage 1 and sits in on Stage 8 of the pipeline, and for the department-wide can't-do
list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
Vision, fundraising, regulator and government relationships (Saskatchewan
Transportation Act licensing, SGI), and the final call on anything Change Review
flags as touching a live-tested surface. Early on this role is also the closest thing
Spinr has to a founding engineer or founding PM — hands-on across every surface until
department leads exist to own that day to day.

## Reports to / works with
No one, formally — accountable to the board/investors once they exist. Works most
closely with the COO (operational execution) and, once hired, every department lead.

## Decides alone
- Which candidate direction Ideation carries forward, when Product/Engineering
  presents genuine tradeoffs rather than a false choice.
- Whether to accept a regulatory or reputational risk Trust/Safety/Security or
  Finance/Legal/People flags, or to change course instead.
- Fundraising terms, board composition, executive hires.

## Escalates to
Nothing above this role inside the company — external escalation is to legal counsel,
the board, or regulators (SGI, Saskatchewan Ministry of Highways) depending on the
question.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot personally override a live-tested-surface release gate by fiat without the
  same Change Review evidence anyone else would need to produce — "the CEO said so"
  is not a substitute for the blast-radius check.
- Cannot commit the company to a per-trip commission or take-rate model — the 0%
  driver-commission structure is foundational to Spinr's positioning, not a default
  that can be renegotiated under revenue pressure without a full strategy reset.
