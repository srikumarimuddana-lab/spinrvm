pipeline and defines the can't-do list its individual roles inherit; each individual
role doc adds only what's specific to that role. See the published org chart artifact
for the phase-by-phase (ideation → scale) headcount story this roster maps to.

---

**A note on the two "one doc, many instances" roles** (Engineering Manager,
City Launch Manager): these are genuinely the same role repeated — a backend EM and
a rider-app EM don't need meaningfully different can't-do lists or escalation paths,
and neither does a Regina launch manager vs. a Saskatoon one. Splitting those into
per-instance files would multiply near-duplicate content without adding real
information; the roster stays complete without inflating the count for its own sake.

**A note on roles that merge earlier-phase and later-phase titles into one doc**
(Insurance / Risk Advisor, Legal Counsel, Privacy / Compliance Lead → DPO): these
represent one person/role maturing as the company scales, not two simultaneous
positions — see each department's own doc for where that evolution happens in the
[org chart](../PIPELINE_DESIGN.md)'s phase story. Data Scientist/ML Engineer and
Corporate Account Executive/Customer Success were split into separate files instead,
because those are genuinely two different simultaneous roles at Scale, not one role
evolving.
