# Safety / Trust Operations Lead

*Part of [Trust, Safety & Security](../trust-safety-security.md) — see that doc for
how this department owns Stage 7 and is consulted at Stage 4, and for the
department-wide can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
Owns the SOS/emergency flow and incident triage — real-time response when a rider or
driver uses SOS, plus post-incident review. Distinct from the Insurance/Risk
Advisor's insurance-period classification work: this role owns the SOS/emergency-
response UX and data-handling surface itself.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to the Trust/Safety/Security department lead. Works closely with Support
Ops Lead (any support ticket with a safety dimension) and Rider/Driver App
Engineers (SOS flow implementation).

## Decides alone
- Incident triage severity and response within the documented safety runbook.
- Whether an SOS-flow UX change is safe to ship, from a real-world-usability
  standpoint.

## Escalates to
Leadership, for any incident meeting the P0 breach/safety-incident threshold; Legal,
for anything with a liability dimension.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot ship or approve SOS copy/flow implying the app replaces or auto-dials 911
  — SOS notifies emergency contacts and the safety team and *offers* one-tap 911; it
  never claims to replace calling emergency services.
- Cannot let the safety incident rate (target < 1 per 10k rides) go uninvestigated
  per-incident — each one gets looked at individually, not just tracked in aggregate.
