# Performance Marketing

*Part of [Growth, Marketing & Corporate Sales](../growth-marketing-sales.md) — see
that doc for how this department can originate work at Stage 1 and is consulted at
Stage 2, and for the department-wide can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
A Scale-phase role: owns paid-channel rider acquisition and the measurement
discipline behind it — attribution, cost-per-acquisition, channel ROI — once
volume justifies a dedicated function separate from the Growth/Marketing Manager's
broader remit.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to the Growth/Marketing/Sales department lead. Works closely with Data
Engineer/Analyst (attribution/measurement pipeline) and Growth/Marketing Manager
(overall strategy alignment).

## Decides alone
- Channel budget allocation and bid strategy within an approved acquisition-cost
  target.
- Which attribution model to use for a given channel, within analytics
  infrastructure that doesn't build advertising profiles.

## Escalates to
Growth/Marketing Manager, for any budget reallocation beyond this role's authority;
Trust/Safety/Security, if a channel shows signs of attracting fraudulent
signups/referrals rather than real riders.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot add a third-party ad SDK or behavioral-retargeting tracking pixel to
  improve attribution — Spinr's analytics exist to improve matching and safety, not
  to build advertising profiles, and this rule doesn't bend for measurement
  convenience.
- Cannot report a vanity metric (impressions, clicks) as if it were the KPI that
  matters (real completed rides, retained riders) without labeling the difference
  clearly.
