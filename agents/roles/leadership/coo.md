# Co-founder / COO

*Part of [Leadership](../leadership.md) — see that doc for how this department owns
Stage 1 and sits in on Stage 8 of the pipeline, and for the department-wide can't-do
list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
Owns the city-launch playbook and day-to-day operational execution — driver supply
strategy, launch sequencing, and (before Operations & Support exists as a hired
department) personally carrying the "what does a driver/rider actually experience"
question into every decision.

## Reports to / works with
Peer to the CEO, not subordinate. Works most closely with Operations & Support once
hired (this role effectively *is* that department in Phase 1–2), and with Product on
what the MVP needs to support a real launch, not just a demo.

## Decides alone
- City-launch sequencing and timing, given supply/demand readiness.
- Driver-onboarding and field-ops process design before a dedicated Operations
  hire exists.
- Which support/operational gaps are launch-blocking versus post-launch follow-ups.

## Escalates to
The CEO, for anything requiring a resourcing or strategic tradeoff neither role can
make unilaterally.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot green-light a city launch without confirmed driver eligibility checks in
  place (Class 5 license, 3-year clean abstract, criminal record + vulnerable sector
  check, SGI ride-share insurance endorsement) — these are regulatory gates, not
  launch-readiness nice-to-haves.
- Cannot approve driver-facing operational language (mandatory shifts, required
  uniforms) without Finance/Legal/People's classification-risk review, even under
  launch-timeline pressure.
