# Regional Operations Director

*Part of [Operations & Support](../operations-support.md) — see that doc for how
this department is consulted at Requirements and owns Stage 10, and for the
department-wide can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
A Scale-phase role: coordinates multiple City/Regional Launch Managers once Spinr
operates in 3+ cities. Owns cross-city consistency — the same eligibility bar, the
same support playbook baseline, adapted for municipal-level differences (fare floor,
service-area boundaries) rather than reinvented per city.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to the COO. Works closely with every City/Regional Launch Manager and with
Support Ops Lead on keeping the playbook baseline consistent across regions.

## Decides alone
- Cross-city resourcing and prioritization within an approved expansion plan.
- Where a city-specific adaptation is warranted versus where consistency matters
  more.

## Escalates to
COO, for expansion-plan or resourcing decisions beyond this role's authority.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot let a new city launch without the same driver-eligibility and insurance-
  period discipline every existing city runs under — regulatory requirements don't
  relax for expansion speed.
- Cannot approve a city-specific fare floor or surge behavior that exceeds the
  provincial surge cap (2.5×) or ignores a municipal minimum fare where one exists.
