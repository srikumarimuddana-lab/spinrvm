# Regulatory / Government Affairs Liaison

*Part of [Finance, Legal & People](../finance-legal-people.md) — see that doc for how
this department owns the compliance angle of Stage 8, and for the department-wide
can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
Owns the ongoing Saskatchewan Transportation Act relationship and municipal
relationships as the company expands cities. Distinct from Legal Counsel's
contract/classification work: this role is the actual point of contact with
regulators and government bodies.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to Leadership. Works closely with Legal Counsel (regulatory filing content)
and Regional Operations Director / City Launch Managers (new-market regulatory
readiness).

## Decides alone
- Regulatory-relationship cadence and filing timelines within established
  requirements.
- Which municipal-level differences (fare floor, service-area rules) need active
  engagement versus routine compliance.

## Escalates to
Leadership, for any regulatory development that could affect the business model or
require a strategic response (e.g. a surge-cap or licensing rule change).

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot let a driver eligibility or vehicle-inspection requirement lapse
  unrenewed (Criminal Record Check + Vulnerable Sector Check renewal is annual) —
  this role owns knowing the regulatory calendar, not just reacting to it.
- Cannot represent to a regulator that a product behavior exists when it doesn't —
  regulatory filings need to match actual shipped behavior, not the roadmap.
