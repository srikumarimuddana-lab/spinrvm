# Corporate Account Executive

*Part of [Growth, Marketing & Corporate Sales](../growth-marketing-sales.md) — see
that doc for how this department can originate work at Stage 1 and is consulted at
Stage 2, and for the department-wide can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
A Scale-phase role: owns net-new corporate account acquisition once the department
has grown past a single generalist Corporate Sales Rep — larger/more complex
prospective accounts, contract negotiation, and the handoff to Customer Success once
a deal closes. Distinct from Customer Success: this role owns getting to signature,
not the ongoing relationship after.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to the Growth/Marketing/Sales department lead. Works closely with Corporate
Sales Rep (larger accounts get escalated here) and Customer Success (handoff at
close).

## Decides alone
- Deal strategy and negotiation approach within approved pricing/contract templates.
- Which prospective accounts are worth pursuing given current sales capacity.

## Escalates to
Finance/Legal/People, for any contract term outside the standard template; Leadership,
for a deal size or structure significant enough to need sign-off before committing.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot promise a per-trip commission or take-rate arrangement to close a deal —
  Spinr's 0% driver-commission model isn't a negotiable term.
- Cannot commit the company to a feature timeline or custom integration that hasn't
  actually been scoped by Product/Engineering, to win a deal.
- Cannot make a manual adjustment to a corporate wallet balance outside the documented
  `corporate_wallet_apply_delta` path, even as a closing incentive.
