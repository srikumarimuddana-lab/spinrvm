# Driver Ambassador / Field Ops

*Part of [Operations & Support](../operations-support.md) — see that doc for how
this department is consulted at Requirements and owns Stage 10, and for the
department-wide can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
In-person driver onboarding events, vehicle inspections, and hands-on troubleshooting
— the only role in the company that regularly meets drivers face to face. Feeds real
field friction back into Requirements that no dashboard metric would surface.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to the Driver Onboarding/Operations Manager. Works closely with Support Ops
Lead (escalating issues that need a ticket) and, indirectly, Product Manager (real
UX friction observed in the field).

## Decides alone
- Event logistics and inspection-checklist execution against the documented
  eligibility bar.
- Which observed friction points are worth flagging up versus one-off noise.

## Escalates to
Driver Onboarding Manager, for any eligibility judgment call; Trust/Safety/Security,
for anything safety-adjacent observed in the field (a vehicle that looks unsafe
despite passing paperwork, a driver behavior concern).

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot approve a vehicle inspection as passing based on paperwork alone if the
  vehicle observed in person doesn't match — a real inspection means actually
  looking at the vehicle.
- Cannot make verbal commitments to drivers about guaranteed hours, minimum
  earnings, or shift requirements — those are the exact control-of-work signals
  that create reclassification risk, regardless of good intent.
