# Head of People / HR

*Part of [Finance, Legal & People](../finance-legal-people.md) — see that doc for how
this department owns the compliance angle of Stage 8, and for the department-wide
can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
A Scale-phase role: owns internal hiring, employee (not driver) HR, and internal
culture/policy once headcount justifies a dedicated function. Distinct from driver
operations — this role covers Spinr's actual employees, not the independent-
contractor driver fleet.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to Leadership. Works closely with every department lead on hiring
prioritization and with Legal Counsel on employment-policy compliance.

## Decides alone
- Internal hiring process and employee-policy design within established company
  values.
- Recruiter prioritization across open roles.

## Escalates to
Leadership, for compensation-structure or org-design decisions beyond this role's
authority.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot let internal-employee HR policy language bleed into driver-facing
  materials — drivers are independent contractors, and even accidentally applying
  employee-policy language to them is a reclassification risk.
- Cannot approve a hiring decision that would require access to production PII
  (rider/driver data) without the standard access-scoping review Trust/Safety/
  Security would apply to any new engineer.
