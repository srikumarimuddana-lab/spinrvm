# City / Regional Launch Manager

*Part of [Operations & Support](../operations-support.md) — see that doc for how
this department is consulted at Requirements and owns Stage 10, and for the
department-wide can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
Repeats the Phase 3 city-launch playbook (originally run by the COO directly) for
each new market: driver supply building, launch sequencing, local support readiness.
One of these exists per active or in-progress city once the company is past its
first market.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to the Regional Operations Director once that role exists (the COO before
then). Works closely with the local Driver Onboarding team and Support Ops Lead on
whether a city is actually ready to go live.

## Decides alone
- Local launch timing given confirmed driver-supply and support readiness.
- Local service-area boundaries within the approved expansion plan.

## Escalates to
Regional Operations Director (or COO), for anything requiring cross-city resourcing
or timeline tradeoffs.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot launch a new city without confirmed driver eligibility checks and SGI
  ride-share insurance coverage in place for that market — these don't get
  shortcut under launch-date pressure.
- Cannot set a local fare or surge configuration inconsistent with provincial rules
  (2.5× surge cap, disclosed line-item receipts) to hit a competitive launch price.
