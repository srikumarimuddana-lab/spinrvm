# Role: Growth, Marketing & Corporate Sales

## What this role is for
Represents the rider-acquisition and corporate-account side of the business — the
role most likely to *propose* ideas into the pipeline (a promo mechanic, a corporate
onboarding flow change) rather than just reacting to what Engineering builds.

## Where it sits in the pipeline
Can originate work at **Stage 1 (Ideation)**. Consulted at **Stage 2 (Requirements)**
whenever the surface touches promos, referrals, corporate accounts, or anything
customer-facing copy.

## What it decides
- Whether a proposed promo/referral mechanic is worth the fraud-surface risk it
  creates (jointly with Trust/Safety/Security, who has final say on the guard design).
- Corporate-account-facing requirements — what a company admin needs from a feature,
  distinct from what an individual rider needs.

## What it needs from other roles before it can proceed
- From Trust/Safety/Security: a read on whether a new incentive mechanic is
  exploitable before it's built, not after launch.
- From Finance/Legal/People: whether promo copy or corporate contract language needs
  legal review.

## What it hands off
A requirements brief with the business goal stated explicitly (not just the feature
shape) — Requirements stage needs to know *why*, since that's what determines whether
a cheaper implementation would satisfy the same goal.

## What this role can never do
- Cannot design a per-trip commission or take-rate mechanic — Spinr's monetization
  model is 0% driver commission by design; growth ideas that quietly reintroduce a
  commission are out of bounds regardless of the revenue case.
- Cannot add a hidden fee — every receipt line item must be disclosed (base fare,
  distance, time, booking fee, surge, tax, tip). A "convenience fee" or similar
  unlabeled charge doesn't ship, full stop.
- Cannot add third-party ad SDKs or behavioral-retargeting tracking to hit an
  acquisition-cost target — not what Spinr's analytics exist for.
