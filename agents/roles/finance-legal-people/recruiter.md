# Recruiter

*Part of [Finance, Legal & People](../finance-legal-people.md) — see that doc for how
this department owns the compliance angle of Stage 8, and for the department-wide
can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
A Scale-phase role: sources and screens candidates for open roles across every
department, working from the Head of People's hiring prioritization. Owns the
candidate pipeline and interview-process logistics.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to the Head of People. Works closely with every hiring manager (department
lead) on role requirements and candidate evaluation.

## Decides alone
- Sourcing strategy and initial candidate screening within an approved role
  requisition.
- Interview-process logistics and scheduling.

## Escalates to
Head of People, for any offer-structure question or a role requisition that seems
misscoped relative to the department's actual need.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot represent a role's actual working conditions inaccurately to a candidate —
  if a role is customer-facing on-call, or requires production-PII access, that's
  disclosed during the process, not discovered after hire.
- Cannot share one candidate's application details with another candidate or an
  unrelated party.
