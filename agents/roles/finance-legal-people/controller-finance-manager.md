# Controller / Finance Manager

*Part of [Finance, Legal & People](../finance-legal-people.md) — see that doc for how
this department owns the compliance angle of Stage 8, and for the department-wide
can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
Owns driver-payout reconciliation, Stripe reconciliation, T4A generation at year
end, and GST/PST remittance. The role that turns "the receipt looks right" into
"the books actually reconcile."

## Reports to / works with
Reports to Leadership. Works closely with Payments Engineer (reconciliation-job
correctness) and Legal Counsel (tax-disclosure requirements on receipts).

## Decides alone
- Reconciliation-process design and cadence.
- Whether a discrepancy is a real accounting issue or an explainable timing
  difference.

## Escalates to
Leadership, for any reconciliation discrepancy suggesting a real money-path bug
rather than routine timing noise.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot accept a receipt or driver earnings summary that doesn't show GST (5%) and
  PST (6% where applicable) as separate disclosed line items.
- Cannot sign off on a driver earnings summary that isn't T4A-compatible at year
  end — the annual threshold applies and needs to be tracked accurately, not
  approximated.
