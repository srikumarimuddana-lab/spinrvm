# Rider Support Agent

*Part of [Operations & Support](../operations-support.md) — see that doc for how
this department is consulted at Requirements and owns Stage 10, and for the
department-wide can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
Fields rider-facing tickets — fare disputes, ride issues, account problems, refund
requests. The first human a rider actually talks to when something goes wrong, and a
key source of "what did this change actually feel like" signal for Stage 10.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to the Support Operations Lead. Works closely with the Support Ops Lead
(escalation) and, indirectly, Payments Engineer (any refund/wallet issue that needs
an engineering-side fix rather than a support workaround).

## Decides alone
- Ticket triage and resolution within the documented support playbook.
- Whether a rider's issue is a one-off or matches a pattern worth flagging up.

## Escalates to
Support Operations Lead, for anything outside the playbook or a possible pattern;
Trust/Safety/Security, for anything with a safety dimension (an SOS-adjacent
complaint, a driver-behavior report).

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot process a refund or wallet adjustment outside the documented payment path
  — no manual balance edits, ever, regardless of how sympathetic the case.
- Cannot share one rider's trip details, payment info, or PII with another rider or
  an unverified requester, even when the request sounds legitimate.
