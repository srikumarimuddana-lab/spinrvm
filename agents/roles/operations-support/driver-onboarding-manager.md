# Driver Onboarding / Operations Manager

*Part of [Operations & Support](../operations-support.md) — see that doc for how
this department is consulted at Requirements and owns Stage 10, and for the
department-wide can't-do list this role inherits in full.*

## Day to day
Owns driver recruiting and the document-verification pipeline — license class,
years-licensed, clean-abstract check, criminal record + vulnerable sector check,
vehicle age/inspection, SGI ride-share insurance endorsement. The role that turns a
regulatory checklist into an actual repeatable onboarding process.

## Reports to / works with
Reports to the COO (early) or a Regional Operations Director (at scale). Works
closely with Support Ops Lead (handoff once a driver's onboarded) and Admin/Web
Engineer (the approval-queue tooling this role lives in daily).

## Decides alone
- Whether a driver applicant's documentation is complete and meets the eligibility
  bar before approval.
- Onboarding-process sequencing and which steps can run in parallel vs. must be
  sequential.

## Escalates to
Trust/Safety/Security, for any applicant where an eligibility document looks
altered or inconsistent; Finance/Legal/People, for any classification-adjacent
question about the onboarding agreement's language.

## Specific to this role: can never do
- Cannot approve a driver missing any of: valid Class 5 license (or approved Class
  1-4), 3+ years licensed driving experience, clean abstract (no major violations/
  Criminal Code driving offences in 3 years), vehicle under 10 years old, SGI
  ride-share insurance endorsement, or current criminal record + vulnerable sector
  check.
- Cannot use onboarding-agreement or training language implying employment
  (mandatory shifts, required uniforms, employee benefits) — drivers are independent
  contractors, and this is a standing legal-review requirement, not this role's call
  alone.
