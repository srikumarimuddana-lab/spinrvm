Design Decision — Winner: C (Arrival Zone UI)

A — Map-Dominant

Stop confidenceLow
Passenger locationNone
Glance-readablePartial

The instruction strip says "your destination is on the right" but doesn't indicate which lane, which curb section, or where the passenger is standing. Driver must visually scan the street while moving — exactly what we want to eliminate.

B — Card-Dominant

Stop confidenceMedium
Passenger locationNone
Glance-readableGood

The big card is legible at a glance — strong for turns. But "drop off on right" is still textual and doesn't convey the exact stop zone. No rider pin means a follow-up call is almost always required at pickup.

C — Arrival Zone (Winner)

Stop confidenceHigh
Passenger locationShown
Glance-readableGood

The amber zone answers WHERE to stop. The pulsing blue pin answers WHERE IS MY RIDER. One glance resolves both questions — no address reading, no phone call. Hold-to-confirm prevents false arrivals. This is the only variant with zero follow-up steps on a clean pickup.
