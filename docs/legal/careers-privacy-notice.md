# Spinr Careers & Recruiting Privacy Notice — Draft for Legal Review

> **What this is.** A PIPEDA notice scoped to job applicants, separate from
> the rider/driver Privacy Policy — applicant data (résumés, references,
> interview notes) is collected for a different purpose than ride-service
> data and deserves its own notice rather than being silently covered (or
> not covered) by the consumer-facing policy. Only relevant if/when Spinr
> has a public careers or job-application page; if none exists yet, this is
> ready to publish alongside one.
>
> **This is a draft, not legal advice**, and has not been reviewed by
> counsel licensed in Saskatchewan or Canada.

---

## BEGIN DRAFT

SPINR CAREERS PRIVACY NOTICE

Last updated: [INSERT PUBLICATION DATE]

This notice explains how Spinr Mobility Inc. ("Spinr," "we," "us")
handles personal information you provide when you apply for a position with
us. It's separate from our rider/driver Privacy Policy, which covers Spinr's
ride-share platform, not our recruiting process.

WHAT WE COLLECT

When you apply for a role, we collect the information you provide —
typically your name, contact information, résumé, cover letter, and any
information you choose to include about your work history and
qualifications. If you reach a later stage in our process, we may collect
references you provide and notes from interviews.

WHY WE COLLECT IT

We use this information solely to evaluate your application, communicate
with you about your candidacy, and, if you're hired, to begin your
onboarding as an employee or contractor.

WHO WE SHARE IT WITH

We share applicant information internally with the hiring team for the role
you applied to, and with [ANY RECRUITING/ATS VENDOR USED — NAME IT HERE, OR
REMOVE THIS CLAUSE IF NONE IS USED] to manage the application process. We do
not sell applicant data or use it for advertising.

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

If you're not hired, we retain your application for [NUMBER, E.G. 12
MONTHS] in case a future role is a better fit, unless you ask us to delete
it sooner. If you're hired, your application materials become part of your
personnel record, retained according to Spinr's employee record-keeping
practices.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask us to access or delete the application information we hold
about you at any time by emailing careers@spinr.ca, except where we're
required to keep a specific record longer (for example, records related to
a role you were ultimately offered).

CONTACT

Questions about this notice can be sent to careers@spinr.ca.

## END DRAFT
